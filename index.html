About This System

🎯 The Goal

Give Sheboygan citizens a voice in government decisions through weekly direct voting on council agenda items.

⚖️ Is This Official?

No. This is citizen input, not legally binding. But transparency creates accountability. When citizens vote 73% NO and council votes YES, that matters.

🔒 Voting Security

Email verification prevents spam. One vote per person per item. All votes are anonymous. Results are public and auditable.

📢 What Happens Next?

We publish citizen results before the council meeting. After the meeting, we publish a comparison showing where citizen and council votes agreed or diverged.

📬 Get Ballot Notifications

New ballots published every week when council agendas drop (usually 48 hours before meetings).
